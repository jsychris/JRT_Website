§ 02 / Our history

Since 1948 adopt, adapt, improve.

Jersey Round Table No. 192 was chartered in 1948, just three years after the Liberation. A small group of close friends, led by Brian Le Marquand - who later became national president of Round Table Britain & Ireland - decided the island needed something. A place for young men to meet, to lift each other up, and to do a bit of good while they were at it.

Nearly eight decades later, the table is still set. The Charity Ball is now an island institution. Members have repainted children's wards, built the steps and walkways of the Governor's Walk at La Gigoulande Mill in St Peter's Valley, and Father Christmassed every parish on the island. Memorable names have sat at this table over the years - the current Bailiff and a past Chief Minister among them - but the brief has stayed the same.

"Adopt, adapt, improve" — the words of King George VI, still our brief.
